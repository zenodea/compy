Compy connects your Android phone, foldable or tablet to your Rex servers.

* Live terminals with full scrollback, drawn by the libghostty-vt engine
* Your real Rex layout, with draggable dividers and pane arranging
* Sessions from every server in one list, with agent status at a glance
* A customizable key bar, a composer for long prompts and voice typing, and hardware keyboard support
* Material You colors, terminal themes, an AMOLED option and importable fonts
* Share text into a pane, plus a widget, Quick Settings tile and app shortcuts
* No telemetry. Compy only talks to your own servers.

Compy is not affiliated with the makers of Rex.
